# Shared mathematical notation

**Status:** author draft for [issue #10](https://github.com/hairer/humanise-yang-mills/issues/10); independent correspondence and editorial review pending. This is the notation contract for proof units and chapters at imported OpenAI-YM revision `3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464`. The [public statement ledger](public-statement.md) determines theorem scope. Symbols here are prose conventions, not a renaming of formal declarations or an assertion that two source structures are definitionally equal.

The entries use targeted inspection of the cited Lean definitions, not an interpretation inferred from names. Scott Armstrong's [glossary at commit `8f3c7cc`](https://github.com/scottnarmstrong/yang-mills-informalization/blob/8f3c7ccaeabb17b0f928bc1a2e75328691de3032/glossary.md) supplied navigation and some mathematical terminology; its `certain`, `probable`, and `tentative` labels are not this project's review status. Credit that project when reusing its source clustering or declaration descriptions. Do not copy a nickname or suggested physical interpretation without checking the definition and the hypotheses of the consuming theorem.

Use the symbols in the **Prose notation** column when an entry is in scope. A unit may choose a local alternative only with the conversion required by [the notation protocol](../docs/notation.md). A symbol outside this table is local to the unit until a dedicated glossary task accepts it.

## Gauge group and spacetime

| Prose notation | Meaning and space | Source correspondence and convention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `G ⊂ U(r_G)` | A closed matrix subgroup in a fixed unitary representation of complex dimension `r_G = n+1`; this is the gauge-group input, not its Lie algebra. | `YangMills.GaugeGroup`, `Lean/PublicTheory.lean:25–34`; internal `YM.Gauge`, `Lean/YM/Gauge/MatrixGroup.lean:20–26`. The representation is part of the formal data. |
| `𝔤 = Lie(G)` | The **real** Lie algebra of matrices whose real-parameter exponentials remain in `G`. | `YangMills.GaugeGroup.lieAlgebra`, `Lean/PublicTheory.lean:25–34`; `YangMills.GaugeGroup.IsSimple`, `:37–45`, requires nonabelian bracket and no proper nonzero real Lie ideal. Internal `YM.Gauge.Simple` is in `Lean/YM/Gauge/Lie.lean:178–182`. `r_G` and `dimℝ 𝔤` are different quantities. |
| `E₄`, `x=(\mathbf x,t)` | Four-dimensional Euclidean coordinate space, with three spatial coordinates first and the time coordinate last. | Public `YangMills.Spacetime`, `Lean/PublicTheory.lean:51–68`; internal `YM.E4`, `Lean/YM/Space/Configurations.lean:24`. Euclidean distance is used for positions and test functions. |
| `\overline V_+` | Closed Minkowski forward cone on the same coordinate vector space: `‖\mathbf x‖ ≤ t`; Minkowski square has sign `t²−‖\mathbf x‖²`. | `YangMills.forwardCone`, `Lean/PublicTheory.lean:62–68`. The Wightman spectrum condition places spectral support in this closed cone; it does not supply a positive mass gap. |
| `\mathscr S(E₄;\mathbb C)` | Complex Schwartz test functions on four-dimensional Euclidean coordinates; product tests retain their stated factors. | `YangMills.TestFunction`, `Lean/PublicTheory.lean:70`; source-specific smooth compact tests are identified separately where used. Do not silently substitute one test space for the other. |

## Finite Wilson lattice and limits

For a public Wilson family write `\mathcal W`. For an internal construction family write `\mathcal C`; its public projection is `\mathcal W(\mathcal C)`. Both are bound as `C` in different Lean declarations, but they carry different fields. This distinction is mandatory at a chapter handoff.

| Prose notation | Meaning and space | Source correspondence and convention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `\mathcal W` | Public Wilson family: period and denominator sequences, bare coefficient, invariant probability Haar measure, token type, and insertion map. | `YangMills.WilsonFamily`, `Lean/PublicWilson.lean:35–46`. `YangMills.wilsonFamily` projects an internal family through `Lean/WilsonBridge.lean:20–30`; an arbitrary internal family is not automatically a proved public theorem witness. |
| `P_p`, `D_N`, `a_N=D_N^{-1}` | `P_p=period(p)` is the physical linear period in the chosen length units; `D_N=denominator(N)` and `a_N` is mesh spacing. Both positive-integer value sequences are strictly increasing. | `YangMills.WilsonFamily.period`, `YangMills.WilsonFamily.denominator`, `YangMills.WilsonFamily.mesh`, `Lean/PublicWilson.lean:35–52`. The finite torus has `\ell_{N,p}=P_pD_N=P_p/a_N` sites per axis, not `P_pa_N`; the suitability box has half-width `P_p/2` (`:75–77`). |
| `\Lambda_{N,p}` | Four-dimensional periodic site set `(\mathbb Z/\ell_{N,p}\mathbb Z)^4`; a link configuration assigns an element of `G` to each site and oriented axis. | `YangMills.Site`, `YangMills.Configuration`, `Lean/PublicWilson.lean:10–13`. A site's physical coordinate uses `a_N` times the symmetric `ZMod.valMinAbs` representative (`:49–52`). |
| `\beta_{N,p}`, `\mu_{N,p}^{\mathcal W}` | Bare plaquette-action coefficient and normalized Wilson law on link configurations. | `YangMills.WilsonFamily.beta`, `YangMills.wilsonEnergy`, `YangMills.wilsonLaw`, `Lean/PublicWilson.lean:20–46`. The law is product Haar weighted by `\exp(-\beta_{N,p}\,\mathrm{wilsonEnergy})` and normalized by its partition integral. Do not use `\mu` for the phase measure in the same chapter. |
| `v∈\mathcal V_{\mathcal W}`, `O_{N,p}(v,z;U)` | A public insertion token and its complex-valued local lattice observable at site `z` in configuration `U`. | `YangMills.WilsonFamily.Token`, `YangMills.WilsonFamily.insertion`, `Lean/PublicWilson.lean:35–46`. `YangMills.WilsonRegularity.localSupport`, `Lean/ChallengeSpec.lean:11–22`, gives one finite offset stencil **per token**, uniform in `N,p,z`. |
| `W_{N,p}(\mathbf v,\mathbf z)` | Finite moment: Wilson-law integral of the product of the chosen insertions. | `YangMills.WilsonFamily.finiteMoment`, `Lean/PublicWilson.lean:54–57`. The smeared `n`-point moment includes `a_N^{4n}` (`:79–81`). |
| `s_{\mathcal W}>0` | Positive asymptotic running slope in `\beta_{N,p}/\log(a_N^{-1})→s_{\mathcal W}` as `N→∞`, uniformly in `p`. | `YangMills.WilsonRegularity.running`, `Lean/ChallengeSpec.lean:11–22`. It is **not** the positive block-kernel penalty `b` quantified in `YangMills.constructed`, nor automatically `b₀(G)`. |
| `\varrho`, `\sigma` | A requested strictly increasing public volume-index sequence and a strictly increasing refinement. The theorem uses one `\mathcal W` before `\varrho`, then may choose `\sigma` and a hierarchy depending on `\varrho`. | `YangMills.JointExistence`, `Lean/ChallengeSpec.lean:186–188`; source binders are `p,q`. Reserve plain `\pi` for the circle constant. |
| `\lim_{k→∞}\lim_{N→∞}` | Public separated-test limit: for each suitable fixed volume row a full mesh limit exists, then rows along `\varrho\circ\sigma` tend to the unselected correlation integral. | `YangMills.WilsonFamily.Converges`, `Lean/PublicWilson.lean:75–90`. The precise statement also requires eventual suitability of the test support; this is not an arbitrary diagonal or unique volume limit. |

## Internal construction and extraction

The internal notation records witnesses needed to build the public family and hierarchy. The source often uses `C,S,F,L,Q,M,X` as local binders. The font changes below prevent collisions with Wilson families, field spaces, and OPE geometry constants in prose.

| Prose notation | Meaning and space | Source correspondence and convention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `\mathcal C` | Internal `YM.Family G`: RG path/history, holonomy data, graded frames/labels, local sections, reference cutoffs, and transfers. | `YM.Family`, `Lean/YM/Source/Construction.lean:24–31`. `\mathcal C\,\mathrm{Makes}` is an **additional predicate** (`:44–62`), not part of the type of every family. The public bridge is `YangMills.wilsonFamily`. |
| `\lambda_{\rm ker}>0` | Positive blocking-kernel penalty, chosen before the construction family in the detailed theorem. | `YangMills.constructed`, `Lean/Construction.lean:15–31`, source binder `b`; `YM.Nonperturbative.existence` uses the same penalty parameter. Keep it distinct from running slope and coupling. |
| `\mathcal L_d`, `F_d` | Reference label space of bounded polynomial-jet grade `d`, and the later quotient field space of the correlation table. | `YM.Labels.Label`, `Lean/YM/Labels/Polynomial.lean:103–120`; `YM.Table.Field`, `Lean/YM/Euclid/Null.lean:74`. Grade bounds jet height; it is not asserted to be a canonical scaling dimension. A chosen coefficient vector `\mathcal C.label\ d` also needs its frame map before identification with a field. |
| `\mathfrak o=(\kappa,\tau)` | Internal extraction order: a strictly increasing mesh-index sequence `\kappa` and a strictly increasing period-index sequence `\tau`; physical volume varies with the period. A refinement composes each component with a further subsequence. | `YM.Extraction.Sequence`, `YM.Extraction.Order`, `YM.Extraction.Order.refine`, `Lean/YM/Extraction/Orders.lean:20–47,60–64`. The public theorem retains the full mesh sequence; do not identify a general internal `\tau` with its public request `\varrho`. In the explicit public assembly bridge, the internal order is built from the full mesh sequence and that request. |
| `\mathrm{late}_{\mathfrak o}` | Eventuality with period-index threshold outermost and a mesh-index threshold that may depend on the period index: `∀ᶠ k, ∀ᶠ j`. | `YM.Extraction.Order.late`, `Lean/YM/Extraction/Orders.lean:47`; its ordered limit is mesh first, then period. `YM.Extraction.Order.fuse` is a filter on `\mathbb N^2` (`Lean/YM/Extraction/Inherited.lean:27`); use its name explicitly rather than treating it as any diagonal limit. |
| `S` | Internal Euclidean correlation table: multilinear moments indexed by arity, grade tuple and positions. | `YM.Table`, `Lean/YM/Euclid/Table.lean:51–69`. | `\mathsf F`, `\mathcal A` | Internal final package and every-character reconstruction package, respectively. | `YM.Spec.Final`, `Lean/YM/Spec/Closed.lean:64–73`; `YM.EveryPhase.Almanac`, `Lean/YM/EveryPhase/Pack.lean:117–132`. They are constructed witnesses, not synonyms for the public `Hierarchy.Properties` record. |
| `Q`, `\mathcal D` | Internal OPE reader and contact-device package. | `YM.OPE.Orbits.Reader`, `Lean/YM/OPE/Orbits/Cuts.lean:17–24`; `YM.Contacts.Devices`, `Lean/YM/Contacts/Tomography.lean:16–26`. | `X=(U_k,\zeta_k,r_k)` | A selected short-distance scaling witness with grade-indexed spin/frame changes, positive factors and radii decreasing to zero. | `YM.Climb.Scaling`, `Lean/YM/Climb/Cells.lean:24–32`; the source calls the positive factor `xi`. Its `r_k` is not the Wilson mesh `a_N`, and no universal scaling sequence is asserted. |

## Reserved distinctions and open questions

- `n` may mean matrix representation parameter (`r_G=n+1`) or correlation arity in the source. Prose uses `r_G` for representation dimension and `m` for arity when both occur. `d` is grade; `\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathfrak g` is color dimension. These are not interchangeable.
- `p` is reused in source files for a period index, an extraction order, and other parameters. Prose uses `p` only locally after declaring its type; the public requested volume sequence is `\varrho`, while a general internal order is `\mathfrak o=(\kappa,\tau)`. Equate `\tau` with `\varrho` only at the specific bridge that constructs that order.
- `\pi` without adornment is the numerical constant. `P_p` is a physical linear period, `\ell_{N,p}` a lattice site count, and `a_N` a spacing. Do not call all three “volume.”
- `\nu_{\rm ph}` is a character measure; `\mu_{N,p}^{\mathcal W}` is a Wilson measure. `\mathcal S^{\rm mix}` is a correlation, not a measure.
- `Q` denotes `YM.OPE.Orbits.Reader` only after qualification; the public hierarchy's OPE data may be discussed through `\mathcal T`. The other `Reader` types in contact namespaces require their own local notation.
- Source-specific numerical parameters and the many RG norm packages are intentionally **not** assigned global symbols here. Their mathematical types and order of choice must be audited in the relevant proof-unit issue before a notation entry is added. Scott's glossary contains probable/tentative readings in those areas; they are leads, not accepted conventions.
- The public/internal bridges for gauge group and Wilson family are inspected above. The precise projection of `\mathsf F,\mathcal A,Q,\mathcal D,X` to public hierarchy fields is explained by the applicable bridge/assembly proof unit; this glossary does not assert a fieldwise equivalence not checked in that unit.
