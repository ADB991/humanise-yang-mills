# A generalized free field as a witness of the public statement (one lemma open)

**Target.** `theorem YangMills.jointExistence_of_freeField : YangMills.JointExistence` (`Main.lean`), proved from a scalar generalized free field with no use of `Solution` or `YangMills.constructed`. The import closure is the three public files, Mathlib, `../Adequacy.lean` and `../GroupIndependence.lean` (for `haarFamily` only). No file of the upstream release other than the three public ones is used or copied. This formalises finding M4 of the G01 review (issue #162). When complete it shows that the public statement is satisfied by a free-type field for every simple group.

**Status (13 November 2026, phase 2): one `sorry` left; not yet a proof.** The whole development is wired into one build. Of the 75 lemmas of `TASKS.md`, 74 are proved; 73 are free of `sorry` in the build, and one (`Psi_local`) depends on the single remaining open lemma, `wightW_local` (locality: exchanging two adjacent letters with spacelike separated test functions), stated in `Theory/WightWLocal.lean`. Consequently `#print axioms YangMills.jointExistence_of_freeField` still reports `[propext, sorryAx, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`, and `AxiomReport.lean` traces the `sorryAx` of every affected declaration to `wightW_local` alone. The partial proof of that lemma is in `Work/wightW_local.lean` (1,572 lines; three isolated gaps: a Fubini and integrability statement, the boundary values of the line functions with derivative labels at spacelike points, and a uniform bound for them); workers are on these.

**What is proved without `sorry`.** `Host.reconstruct` (a host with good word vectors gives a `WightmanTheory` with `Axioms`); every field of `Host.Good` for the Fock host except locality: the inner-product formula for word vectors via the cut identity, linearity, adjoint, temperedness, Poincaré covariance, the spectral representation, positive energy, uniqueness of the vacuum, faithfulness; all of `Reconstructed` except what needs the theory itself (permutation symmetry, smoothness, analytic continuation, `root`, `boundary`); both clauses of `OperatorProductExpansion` for one coefficient family (`exists_ope_coefficient`); and the two analytic facts behind `TraceTest` (`Sr_asymptotic` with the constant `384π`, `wickProfile_excluded`). `TraceTest` and `Reconstructed` for the hierarchy then depend on the `sorry` only through the existence of the theory.

**Design** (`DESIGN.md`). One scalar field with one-particle measure `weight(p) dp`, `weight = (p²)²/log²(e+p²)` on the forward cone. Labels are the free vector space on multisets of derivative multi-indices (Wick monomials), filtered by grade. The Hilbert space is the closed span of explicit word vectors inside an unsymmetrised direct sum of `L²` spaces. Correlations, their continuation and the Wightman functions are explicit sums over matchings. The trace field is a multiple of the basic field, normalised so that the limit in `TraceTest.shortDistance` is exactly `3 dim 𝔤/π⁴`. The final witness is `Adequacy.hier` applied to this seed hierarchy, so the lattice clause is met as in `../Adequacy.lean`.

**Layout** (`CONSOLIDATION.md` has the details). `Labels`, `TwoPoint`, `Wick`, `Hierarchy`, `Fock`, `Symmetry`: definitions. `Analytic/`, `Reconstruction/`, `Theory/`, `Continuation/`, `OPE/`: one module per lemma or lemma group, in dependency order, each containing the proof and the original statement; `Analytic.lean`, `Theory.lean` and so on re-export them, so the statements and `Main.lean` are those of phase 1 unchanged. `Proofs/`: the two trace lemmas. `AxiomReport.lean`: prints the axioms of the main theorem and of all 75 lemmas and fails the build if any declaration depends on anything other than the three standard axioms or, through `wightW_local` only, on `sorryAx`. `TASKS.md`: the original task table. `Check/check_conventions.py`: numeric tests of the conventions. `reviews/`: the four GPT-6 Astra reviews of the design and statements made before proof work. About 17,000 lines of Lean in 72 files.
