## Outcome

- **Mathematics: accepted locally, conditional on stated dependencies; no required change** ([mathematics.md](mathematics.md)). The proof of `rem:headline-nonconstant-sections` is correct step by step, including d = 0 and d = 1, and its conclusion is no stronger than its proof. The jet normalization, the insertion, the preamble, items 1–4, the closing sentence, the scope paragraph and the changed lines of `statement.tex` are correct as statements, read with the source, apart from M1; as prose, the weaker-target sentence needs the clauses of M5. One Should point (M1): item 1 does not say at which grade a label is inserted and quotiented, and in the source the same polynomial in general gives different fields at different grades. Minor points M2–M10 concern wording, terminology, notation and cross-references.
- **Lean correspondence: accepted locally, conditional on stated dependencies; no required change** ([lean-correspondence.md](lean-correspondence.md)). Every clause of the headline, the χ_* sentence on its existential reading (L1), is carried by `YangMills.constructed` at the pin, either as a field of a structure it asserts or as a short consequence of such fields. No clause states a hypothesis the source lacks, and no non-claim of the scope paragraph denies anything the source proves. One Should point (L1): the sentence selecting χ_* (l.136–137) matches the source on its existential reading; on the reading that any character with the property of `ex-pure-ground` may be chosen, item 4 asserts the trace clauses at characters where the source does not. One Minor point on the author record (L2): crosswalk row 10 does not name the declarations that carry the literal trace constant 3·dim 𝔤/π⁴ and the finite mass-shell exclusion.

The verdicts are conditional on the cited results this review does not establish: the producer chapters, the assembly obligations listed in the scope paragraph and in #439, and the hypotheses of the cited conditional theorems (`ex-pure-ground`, `thm:trace-asymptotic`, `lem:trace-normalized-stress`, `ex-conditional-existence`).

## Findings

Labels: Required / Should / Minor. Identifiers: M = mathematics, L = Lean correspondence, C = checked and correct, N = not checked. No Required point.

- **M1 (Should; l.87–88 and the display l.92–97).** "`Write $[v]$ for the field represented by $v$`" and "`For every finite list $v_1,\ldots,v_n$`" do not give the grade of each label. The spaces V_d are nested (l.11–12), while the insertion (l.33–34, through a_N^{-d}, L_d and R_{d,N,p}) and the quotient F_d depend on d. In the source every label is a token ⟨d, v⟩ (`Token`, `Euclid/Register.lean:88`), and the unit token at grade d is ⟨d, a_{r_d}^d·e₀⟩ (`scalar`, `one`, `Register.lean:28, 90`, with `book` at `Extraction/LatticeFamily.lean:20`), on which `Table.Basic.unital` (`Euclid/Table.lean:77–78`) acts as the unit; the unit field is `Table.unit d`, the class of `scalar d` (`Euclid/Null.lean:100`), and `DiffPlan.fixs` (`Euclid/Differential.lean:95`) sends `S.unit d` to `S.unit e`. So the constant polynomial inserted at grade d represents a_{r_d}^{-d} times the unit, and in general the same polynomial represents different fields at different grades. The grade maps `DiffPlan.rise` (`Euclid/Differential.lean:21`) are constrained by equal correlations (`Inclusions.kernels`, :58–60) and by compatibility with conjugation and mirrors (`Orientation.realInc`, `.mirrorInc`, :67–70), not by a clause sending a label to the same label at a higher grade (a grep; lean-correspondence.md). The weaker target already carries the grade ("Tokens map onto the disjoint union", statement.tex l.98). Suggested: write `$I^{(d)}_{N,p}$` in `\eqref{eq:headline-insertion}` (l.33); at l.87–88, "`For $v\in V_d$ write $[v]_d\in\mathcal F_d$ for its class; the unit of $\mathcal F_d$ is the class of the constant $a_{r_d}^{d}$`"; at l.88, "`For every finite list of labels $v_i\in V_{d_i}$`"; in the display l.95–96, `$I^{(d_i)}_{N,p(q(k))}(v_i;x_i;U)$` and `$S^{\rm mix}_{[v_1]_{d_1},\ldots,[v_n]_{d_n}}$`.
- **L1 (Should; l.136–137, used at l.148–158).** "`Select a character $\chi_*$ as in Theorem~\ref{ex-pure-ground}`" can be read as a choice left to the reader. The source states the trace clauses of item 4 only at `selected F := F.α.ensemble F.α.atPoint` (`TraceExtension/WilsonTrace.lean:47`; `Headline.TraceExclusions`, `Nonperturbative/HeadlineSpec.lean:84–94`), where `atPoint` is a data field of `OS.Selection` (`Phase/Mixture.lean:132`) supplied inside the existential of `YangMills.constructed`. Its recorded property is `Certificate.heat` (`Mixture.lean:148`), a one-dimensional ground space; `EveryPhase.Completed.euclid` (`EveryPhase/Extension.lean:54`, asserted through `Independent.Core`) gives the same property at every character. So the property of `ex-pure-ground` does not single χ_* out, and on the free-choice reading item 4 would assert the trace limit at every character, which the source does not state. The frame "one common system ... with the following properties" (l.79–80) makes the matching reading available. That the source selects by the ground space alone is already on record in the #403 audit (<https://github.com/hairer/humanise-yang-mills/issues/382#issuecomment-6880778008>: "selects the phase by the one-dimensional ground space alone"; its next sentence, "The trace short-distance law is then proved for every `Final`.", concerns each `Final` F, whose law is stated at `selected F := F.α.ensemble F.α.atPoint`); the point here is the quantifier of l.136. Suggested: "`There is a character $\chi_*\in X$, with one-dimensional ground space as in Theorem~\ref{ex-pure-ground}, for which the conclusions below hold; all of them use these same data.`"
- **M2 (Minor; l.15 with l.36–37).** l.15 fixes coordinates only by the unit condition, while D_t multiplies "`a coordinate with $s$ curvature factors`" by t^s, which needs each basis vector homogeneous in the number of curvature factors. The source frame requires each basis vector real and of one bidegree (`Labels.Frame.realForm`, `.pure`, `Labels/Basis.lean:55, 57`; `Frame.scaled`, `Labels/Reference.lean:58–59`), and `local-sections.tex` l.91–92 uses a "real homogeneous basis label". Suggested l.15: "`Fix a real basis of $V_d$, each element homogeneous in weight and in the number of curvature factors, with the constant polynomial as the unit coordinate.`"
- **M3 (Minor; l.30–34).** The cutoff (N,p), the spacing a_N and the configuration U are used before they are introduced (U is not introduced in the subsection at all): a_N first appears at l.33 and is introduced at l.71 ("`one sequence of lattice spacings $a_N\downarrow0$`"); a_N = D_N^{-1}, P_p and the lattice of P_pD_N sites are given at statement.tex l.61–65, after the `\input` at statement.tex l.57. Suggested at l.30: "`Here $N$ is the mesh index and $p$ the period index of the family fixed in Theorem~\ref{thm:ex-construction-headline}, $a_N$ is its lattice spacing and $U$ is a link configuration.`"
- **M4 (Minor; l.101–102).** The sum is referred to `ex-row-target`–`ex-volume-target` (statement.tex l.175, l.179), which state limits of W_{N,p}. The sum over "its fundamental box" (l.102), the "centered fundamental cell" (statement.tex l.91–92) and "suitable" periods (used at l.103–104) are defined at `ex-smearing` and the sentence after it (statement.tex l.86–93), which the headline does not cite. Suggested: "`in its centered fundamental cell, as in \eqref{ex-smearing}; the limits are those of \eqref{ex-row-target}--\eqref{ex-volume-target}.`"
- **M5 (Minor; l.187–189).** "Forgetting ... leaves the weaker target below" holds for the Lean statements (`(hierarchy F Q).Properties` is a conjunct of `YangMills.constructed`, `Construction.lean:22`, and `Solution.lean` projects `YangMills.jointExistence` from it with b = 1). As prose, the weaker target also asks for correlations "`permutation symmetric, and continuous in \(\chi\)`" (statement.tex l.104–105) and for "one centered field of grade four" (statement.tex l.187), which the headline does not state; l.153 implies the grade without stating it. #439's required work item 5 ("Assembly and projection.") includes "Derive the old public target as a consequence." Of these, the permutation symmetry of S^mix follows from the display l.92–97 and that of each S^χ from the locality of item 3; continuity in χ is not stated. Suggested: add "`continuous in $\chi$`" to item 2 and "of grade four" to Θ in item 4, or write "Forgetting ..., and deriving the symmetry, continuity and grade clauses, leaves ...".
- **M6 (Minor; l.70).** "`every positive blocking penalty $b$`" is the first use of the term in reading order, and nothing points to where it is defined; statement.tex l.211 and `lattice.tex` l.43 only name it. `flat-step.tex` l.325–326 introduces "`a positive blocking penalty $\lambda$`" of the blocking map; whether that is the b of l.70 was not checked. Suggested: a forward reference at l.70 to the section the author takes to define the penalty.
- **M7 (Minor, optional; l.79, l.105 against l.16, l.87–88).** The letter v is a label (l.16, l.87–88, l.95–96) and the mesh refinement map in "`$(u\circ v,p\circ q)$`" (l.79) and "`refined mesh $u\circ v$`" (l.105); both occur in item 1. This is the same kind of point as nit 3 of the earlier review, which covers p ("This is readable but overloaded."). Suggested: another letter for the mesh map, for example `$(u\circ w,p\circ q)$`.
- **M8 (Minor, optional).** `ex-pure-ground`, cited at l.136, and its chapter write μ and Ξ for the headline's ν and X (`phase-selection.tex` l.13–18, l.127).
- **M9 (Minor, optional; l.162–164).** The source proves the trace limit outright, while the cited `thm:trace-asymptotic` is conditional ("none of its four hypotheses is proved in this section", `trace-asymptotic.tex` l.336–337), one hypothesis being `input:trace-scales`. The "see ..." is attached to the use of the same normalized tensor; a reader could take it as citing a proof of the limit. Already disclosed at l.8–9 ("whose outstanding hypotheses must still be supplied in the final assembly") and in README obligation 4 ("the trace asymptotic on this very hierarchy"); no change required. Optional: "`see Lemma~\ref{lem:trace-normalized-stress} and Theorem~\ref{thm:trace-asymptotic}, whose hypotheses include Input~\ref{input:trace-scales}.`"
- **M10 (Minor; l.125 against l.64).** Item 3 asserts "a nontrivial local relativistic field theory" at every character; in Definition `ex-reconstructed` the clause is "at least one smeared field is nonzero" (statement.tex l.139–140), which the unit label already gives (lean-correspondence.md, the "nontrivial" row, P4). The remark uses the word for a property of a continuum limit, beside non-Gaussianity: "It does not by itself prove nontriviality or non-Gaussianity of a continuum limit" (l.63–64). With both uses in one subsection, item 3 can be read in the remark's sense, which is stronger than the source's clause. Suggested at l.63–64: "It does not by itself prove that a continuum limit is interacting or non-Gaussian." Optional, at l.125–126: "reconstruct a local relativistic field theory, nontrivial in the sense that at least one smeared field is nonzero, on a Hilbert space".
