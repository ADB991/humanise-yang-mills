# G04 historical source audit, 2026-11-13

The outline uses these papers as historical context, not as proofs of its open construction inputs. The journal originals were inspected at the locators below; this is not an independent mathematical or Lean review of the outline.

- K. G. Wilson, “Confinement of quarks,” Physical Review D 10 (1974), 2445–2459, [original scan](https://hep.physics.uoc.gr/Erasmus-IP-2013/files/lectures/Rabinovici/Wilson.pdf), §III, pp. 2451–2452, (3.28)–(3.29): lattice plaquette action and group integration. The manuscript's conventions and normalization must be read on their own terms; no equality of coupling conventions is claimed.
- D. J. Gross and F. Wilczek, “Ultraviolet behavior of non-Abelian gauge theories,” Physical Review Letters 30 (1973), 1343–1346, [reproduced journal page, PDF slide 11](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/gross-slides.pdf): ultraviolet asymptotic freedom and perturbative logarithmic corrections. The coordinator additionally inspected the [original journal PDF](https://journals.aps.org/prl/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevLett.30.1343), pp. 1343–1344, for the flow and cubic term. Neither establishes the uniform bare lattice estimate claimed here as a separate input.
- H. D. Politzer, “Reliable perturbative results for strong interactions?”, Physical Review Letters 30 (1973), 1346–1349, [original scan](https://www.phenix.bnl.gov/WWW/publish/zajc/sp/presentations/UCSB/Politzer.pdf), pp. 1346–1348, especially (4), (7)–(8): negative perturbative beta function and inverse-log running in the stated regime, including the pure-gauge case. This is motivation, not the uniform construction estimate.
- T. Bałaban, “Renormalization group approach to lattice gauge field theories. I. Generation of effective actions in a small field approximation and a coupling constant renormalization in four dimensions,” Communications in Mathematical Physics 109 (1987), 249–301, [journal scan](https://scispace.com/pdf/renormalization-group-approach-to-lattice-gauge-field-3ygai0vxt1.pdf), abstract and §0, pp. 249–251: Wilson renormalization group, four-dimensional pure gauge theory, localized effective actions and recursive coupling, restricted to small fields. No theorem from this paper is invoked by the outline.
- K. Osterwalder and R. Schrader, “Axioms for Euclidean Green's functions,” Communications in Mathematical Physics 31 (1973), 83–112, [original](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/communications-in-mathematical-physics/volume-31/issue-2/Axioms-for-Euclidean-Greens-functions/cmp/1103858969.pdf), pp. 88–90, theorem and positivity/semigroup reconstruction. Their [1975 sequel](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/communications-in-mathematical-physics/volume-42/issue-3/Axioms-for-Euclidean-Greens-functions-II-with-an-Appendix-by/cmp/1103899050.pdf), p. 282 and §IV.1 p. 287, corrects the earlier sufficiency argument and gives revised hypotheses. Neither theorem is asserted as the proof of the present construction.

The main bibliography does not currently include Wilson74, GrossWilczek73, Politzer73, Balaban87RG1 or OS73. F01 must add checked bibliographic entries when including the outline. The temporary review build supplies entries only in its external wrapper.
