## Result and scope

The new subsections in `selected-centered-service.tex` prove two missing joins,
without claiming to construct the entire arrival receipt:

1. `lem:selected-menu-alphabet`: the shipped finite menu augments the actual
   optional alphabet by the sum of all wages mapping to each label. Its rooted
   price increases by at most the shipped allowance. The exact product expansion
   is over subsets on which the label map is injective. Coincident labels cost
   their sum, not an extra factor for the size of the menu. The conditional
   mixed-component estimate then applies with the augmented vertex price.
2. `lem:selected-response-payments`: explicit choices of the two absorption
   parameters pay the ordinary and raised precursor and final rows uniformly
   in all admitted resets. The stage thresholds are chosen before the history;
   they apply on the direct stored doors, including step zero. The raised final
   demand is evaluated at the base budget, although its output receipt has the
   raised exponent. The ordinary and raised demands are not identified.

and the packet/optional hypotheses of the mixed-component result. Its integral
argument bounds all insertion factors pointwise, then integrates once against
the same body measure. It does not multiply separate integral bounds or assume
independence of Gaussian reads. The numerical lemma retains the already stated
definitions of the two soft demands and uses their proved footprint bounds.
