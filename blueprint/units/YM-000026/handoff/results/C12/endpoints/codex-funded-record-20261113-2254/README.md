## Which history?

**Supplied** by `prop:ex-lattice-selected-course-binding` and `prop:ex-original-history-attachment`: once the purchased continuation and the stored run arguments are given, specializing the continuation preserves the actual family, local sections, reference levels and transfer data. In `Lean/YM/FullVolume/Cross.lean`, `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Legato.Run.ramp` selects the alloy, bolt and reference map, then obtains one door/transfer family. `Run.again` specializes `R.bought : Ahead R.b R.Q` to the nine stored arguments and projects `Ties.scrim` on `R.family` and `R.doors`. Here `Ahead` is the `Unlatched` continuation in `Forge.lean` and `Unlatch.lean`. The declaration used in the proof is `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Legato.ahead` (in Forge.lean), whose output includes `Scrim`. The proof does not call `ramp` again.

There are two source presentations. `YM.MergeProof.Scrim` (Biscuit.lean) has `costs : Geyser` plus occupancy lower bound, strict height positivity, the stronger height jump and local tones. `YM.MergeProof.Geyser` (Badlands.lean) contains Moonlit retention, room, bank, Brine, monotone occupancy, heights and Freightage. Its `pond` projection (FullVolume/Fields/Pond.lean) restricts the existing record to a reference level. In contrast `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Legato.Campaign.ponds` (FullVolume/Shroud/Pool.lean) rebuilds retention/transfer pieces for a Cantata and Album, and only for reference levels at least one. An arbitrary Album has its own transfer field and Cantata history. For the stamped original one, `Campaign.visible`, `stamped` and `billbook` (Shroud/Clan.lean) give the original family. The campaign door uses the gate of its actual positive-level step and a default at zero. Equality of families alone is insufficient for an equality of door fields; the direct `Run.again` projection already comes with the stored doors at every level. `Campaign.ridgeT` (Dusk/Cargo.lean) packages the campaign-side Ponds and stronger Mauve outputs.

Fix this one family and indexed door field before all consumers. Reference admissibility is the stored `t_R(d)` (with the public positive reference and coarse-clock grade condition, propagated by monotonicity), not an independently chosen scale. Stock heights have quantifiers `∀ d,k,N,p, t_R(d)≤k≤N, ∃ X`; the camera witness can be fixed in grade when its section is fixed. Music is chosen once for each `(d,N,p)` with `t_R(d)≤N`, then held fixed across labels, motions, censuses and intervening scales. The case `N=t_R(d)` still has its terminal voice; it has no intervening transitions. The birth moment requires `k<N`; service can retain its terminal data when `k=N`. Period and volume index stored entries of one history. No period-specific or volume-specific selection of the underlying run is licensed.
