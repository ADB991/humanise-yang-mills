## M1: cross-reference for the exact-form identity of the amendment

`manuscript/existence/quadratic-lattice-symmetry.tex` (old line 109) attributed the positive
amendment to `sec:curvature-horizon-producer`. The amendment is `eq:positive-amendment` in
`sec:positive-row-producer`. The identity used is proved immediately after it:
`X H d_1 = d_2 d_1 = 0` gives `A H d_1 = d_1`, hence `G d_1 = H d_1`.
These are identities of Laurent rows, and so they also hold for their periodizations on every period.

The paragraph now cites `eq:positive-amendment` and `sec:positive-row-producer`. It also names the
dictionary: the symmetry section's unamended row `P` is the pulse row `H`, and the amended metric
`M` is `G`. That `P = H` was checked against the pulse formula
`pulse_v = rho_v^{-1} M_2(D_v^{-1})` in `free-kernel-estimates.tex` (around line 413). Its diagonal
entry at `p=(a,b)` is `rho^{-1} D_a^{-1} D_b^{-1}`, which is the symbol displayed for `P` in this section.
From `G d_1 = H d_1` we get `d_1^T G_n d_1 = d_1^T H_n d_1`, that is `d^*M_nd = d^*P_nd`.
The rest of the argument is unchanged. The new sentences use no input that the section did not already use.

`curvature-horizon-producer.tex` uses the same identity (its line ~116 cites
`eq:positive-amendment-intertwine` and `Gd_1=Hd_1`). It does not prove it, so the old citation was
a pointer to a consumer, not to the producer.
