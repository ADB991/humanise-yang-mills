This puts every endpoint of every stencil strictly between the reflection planes, uniformly in p; spatial endpoints also remain in the centered physical box. Each smeared observable is a finite sum of bounded Borel gauge-invariant functions, supported on links with both endpoints in the strict positive sector. Applying `reflected_positive` to (F=\sum c_\nu A_{\nu,N,p}) and expanding gives the full finite matrix PSD, with the displayed (a_N^{4(n_\nu+n_\mu)}) factor and normalized Wilson law; no partition/volume factor is introduced. The finite source condition `YM.Cells.Positive` is exactly universal in F, so no extra finite-matrix theorem is needed. The later ordered-limit/extraction passage is explicitly separate; source interface `YangMills.WilsonFamily.Converges` is a full mesh limit at each suitable fixed period followed by a selected volume subsequence, not a diagonal limit.
