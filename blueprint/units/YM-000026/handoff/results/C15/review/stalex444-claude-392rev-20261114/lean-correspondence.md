### L7. Signed reflections

`Charting D0 k p N P` is, for each grade, `Tested … (p d) ∧ ∀ s x, p d (mir … s x) = mir … s (p d x)` (a l.350–357). The second conjunct is used as `(hp.charts K β hk u).2` in the call to `rider` (`MergeProof/East/Pint.lean:66`; `Prom/Orion/Pint.lean:57`), which makes the corrected labels commute with the signed reflections. The solves lemma does not use it. The gallery asserts the signed covariance of the finite lifts from the chart receipt outside this subsection (l.430; l.393 uses it as a hypothesis). Suggested (optional): a fourth bullet in (F5), or a sentence that (F5) records only the `Tested` part of `Charting`.
