## Findings

Labels: Required / Should / Minor. Identifiers: M = mathematics, L = Lean correspondence, C = checked and correct, N = not checked.

- **M1 (Required; 395rev M1, standing).** Lines l.197–243 are byte-identical to `29f27cdb` l.188–234; l.204 still reads "for every $N$ with". With the merged fields, for a chart satisfying the (F1) depth with μ_k > 1 and c_k^{10} > 8(Z_d+2)/3, every N with ℓ ≤ N < k satisfies the mesh condition, and by item 1 of the clock-fields lemma this covers every clock chart at large k, for each fixed d. Suggested repair, as in 395rev: "for every $N\ge k$ with" (or $N>k$). (G4) and its consumer (comparison l.72, l.235) are unaffected. In the source: 395rev L5.
- **M2 (Should).** Add to (F5), or to the lemma, ref(d′) ≤ k for every d′ ≤ d. The Lean witness carries it as `PoisedSky.early` (`Prom/Orion/Plume.lean:24`; Lean paths as in lean-correspondence.md), a field beside `charts` and `supply`; the author record's (F5) row ("`PoisedSky.charts` (`Charting` ⇒ `Tested`) and `supply`", l.27) and the 19:22 comment ("PoisedSky.charts and supply") do not name `early`. The funded chart receipt supplies witnesses only at sufficiently large levels (gallery l.19), so a reader can restore the hypothesis by enlarging that threshold; and outside the subsection the one-step chart output "covers all reference grades through $d$" (gallery l.40–42; `TimberSky.early`, `Prom/Zoom/Boats.lean:44`). Neither (F5) nor the proof cites either. Unlike M1, whose gap is present at every large k, this gap is confined to finitely many levels; hence Should.
- **M3 (Should; expository).** Suggested: make the Status paragraph (l.182–186) say that, for the clock and all sufficiently large k, (F1), (F2) and (F4) are supplied by the clock-fields lemma, the remark after it and `prop:rank-root-output-ticket`, leaving (F3) and (F5) as inputs; adjust l.83–84 to count the three lemmas. Optional: add "for all sufficiently large $k$" to the remark at l.338.
- **M4 (Minor).** The "stamp duty" of (F5) (l.146) is not defined in the manuscript; `actual-rank-purchases.tex` l.436–437 lists it as still to be established. "the clock factors" (l.113) are first given meaning at l.337–338.
