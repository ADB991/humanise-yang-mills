## Hall citation checked against the book text

The title page identifies the second edition and ISBN 978-3-319-13467-3; the [publisher chapter record](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-13467-3_3) independently confirms the edition's chapter and pages 49–75. The theorem characterizes group membership near identity by membership of the local matrix logarithm in the Lie algebra; the corollary gives a local exponential homeomorphism. This supplies the small X tending to 0 needed in the revised application. The fixed gauge group is closed in U(N), hence in GL(N,C); its colour algebra uses the same exponential definition. Finite-dimensional norm equivalence permits a smaller Frobenius neighborhood. Uniqueness in the ambient split chart then gives its colour component X. The citation therefore applies without assuming a global logarithm or a connected group.
