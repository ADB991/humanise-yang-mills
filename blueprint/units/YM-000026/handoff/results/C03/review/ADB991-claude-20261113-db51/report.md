## Mathematical review, step by step

Line numbers refer to `manuscript/existence/phase-fields.tex` at the head. Equation numbers are those in the 985-page build (154–157).

1. **Setting (l.136–170).** Raw spaces A_d are finite dimensional. S is multilinear in labels, smooth on Conf_m and symmetric. An exterior has n raw spectators of arbitrary grades and n+1 distinct positions, with n = 0 allowed. N_d = ker 𝓡_d is nullity against every exterior. J_d : E_d → ℂ^𝒳 is well defined and injective because N_d is exactly the kernel. Correct.
2. **Input (154), l.172–187.** The representative b_{de,j} is quantified before the spectator tuple and the test, and the input is stated for basis vectors only. This is weaker than what the source supplies (see O2), so the conditional result is at least as strong as needed. Correct as a hypothesis.
3. **Raw map and linear extension (l.204–211).** For fixed spectators and test, both sides of (154) are integrals of continuous compactly supported functions. They are linear in the inserted label because S is linear in that slot pointwise and the sum is finite. So (154) holds for every a ∈ A_d with b replaced by K_{de}a. Correct.
4. **Tested ⇒ pointwise (l.213–236).** h is continuous on the open set Conf_{n+1}. Choose c with |c| = 1 and ch(x₀) = |h(x₀)|, and a ball with closure in Conf on which Re(ch) > |h(x₀)|/2. For a nonnegative bump ψ supported there with ∫ψ > 0, the integrand ψ(Re(ch) − |h(x₀)|/2) is continuous, nonnegative and not identically zero. Hence Re(c∫ψh) > (|h(x₀)|/2)∫ψ > 0, which contradicts ∫ψh = 0. A real-valued ψ is an admissible complex test. This gives (155): 𝓡_e K_{de} = 𝓡_d on all exteriors, with no claim at coincident points. Correct. (The source uses Mathlib's `ae_eq_zero_of_integral_contDiff_smul_eq_zero` in `YM.Test.weak_null`. Proving it directly is reasonable here.)
5. **Descent, independence and faithfulness (l.238–252).** a ∈ N_d gives 𝓡_e K a = 0, so K a ∈ N_e and ι_{de} is well defined. Two raw maps with property (155) differ by a map into N_e. (156) J_e ι_{de} = J_d gives injectivity, since J_d is injective, and uniqueness among response-preserving maps, since J_e is injective. Correct.
6. **Every slot (l.254–273).** Symmetry under simultaneous permutation, applied at a point of Conf_m, moves any slot to the head. The result is a head insertion against a raw exterior (m ≥ 1 is automatic when a slot exists). Null labels therefore vanish in every slot. Replacing representatives one slot at a time gives well-defined multilinear quotient correlations. (157) follows from (155) at the head, and then the same permutation. Raising several slots, possibly to different grades, is repeated application. Correct; this is the content of `YM.DiffPlan.inclusion_slot` with T = S.
7. **Identity and composition (l.275–283).** J_d ι_{dd} = J_d and J_f ι_{ef} ι_{de} = J_e ι_{de} = J_d = J_f ι_{df}, using (156) twice. Injectivity of J_d and J_f concludes. The remark that the raw maps need not compose is apt. Correct.
8. **Union (l.296–327).** By (156), J_d(E_d) ⊆ J_e(E_e) for d ≤ e, so the union of an increasing chain of subspaces of ℂ^𝒳 is a subspace. The closure computation with D = max(d, e) is right. Common grade for finitely many elements: D = max dᵢ, or D = 0 for the empty family. Well-definedness of correlations: two common-grade representatives become equal in E_L after raising, by injectivity of j_L, and (157) shows raising does not change the value. Multilinearity, symmetry and smoothness are evaluated at one common grade. Correct. This is the union `YM.Table.observations` = ⨆ range(spectra d), which equals the plain union once the ranges are increasing (`YM.DiffPlan.covered`).
9. **Universal property (l.329–341).** Well-definedness, additivity and homogeneity at a common grade, and uniqueness from coverage are all correct for complex-linear F_d. See O1 on semilinear maps.
10. **Two tables (l.344–363).** N^S_d ⊆ N^T_d makes q_d well defined and surjective. If the same K_{de} satisfies (154) for T, then ι^T_{de} = π^T_e K_{de} on representatives, and q_e ι^S_{de} = ι^T_{de} q_d on π^S_d(A_d) = E^S_d. The induced union map comes from the universal property applied to j^T_d q_d. With equal null spaces, the inverses q_d^{-1} are compatible, since ι^S q_d^{-1} = q_e^{-1} ι^T. T-correlations of S-classes are well defined by containment (`YM.Table.agrees`), and their inclusion compatibility is (155) for T, transported to every slot by symmetry of T. The closing caveat is right: null-space equality alone gives no cross-grade identity. Correct.

No circularity: the argument uses only (154), smoothness, symmetry and multilinearity of the fixed table. It does not use the E6 unit law (PR #265), C04, or any selected-phase result.

Literature: the only external facts are the bump-function argument (proved in the text) and the algebraic direct limit of vector spaces, whose universal property is proved in full. A citation is not needed. The author's search record (`correspondence.md`, last paragraph) is adequate for an elementary construction like this.

## Findings

**N1 (minor, notation within one section).** `ex-no-new-null` (l.59–100) and the paragraph after it use E for a *raw* label space, V = E/N for its quotient, W for the comparison field space and J_k : W → E for corrected labels. The new subsection, in the same section, uses E_d for *quotients*, E for their union, J_d for response maps and W for the target of the universal property. A reader moving from Proposition 39.2 to 39.3 meets four letters whose meaning changes. The new notation agrees with C04 (E_d, E), so the cheaper fix is to rename the symbols in `ex-no-new-null` (for example raw space A, comparison map C_k). Alternatively, add one sentence at the start of the subsection saying the letters are reused. This does not affect correctness.

**N2 (minor, attachment wording).** In `handoff.md` ("Proposed E4 attachment"), injectivity, ι_{dd} = 1 and composition are credited implicitly to `prop:phase-field-union` ("supplies E4(3): injective common level maps…"). In the C03 ledger, E4(3) is "injectivity, transitivity, ι_dd = 1 and the union", and the first three are proved in `prop:phase-field-inclusions`. When the ledgers are patched, the wording should be "`prop:phase-field-inclusions` supplies the inclusion maps of E4(2) and the injectivity/identity/composition part of E4(3); `prop:phase-field-union` supplies the union part of E4(3)". The C04 E4 row also asks for "mixed and selected correlations compatible with the inclusions". The attachment should say that the selected (and mixed) compatibility is the two-table paragraph, and is conditional on (154) for that table (Pantry `habits`), not on `rises`.

**O1 (optional, semilinear universal property).** Source `YM.DiffPlan.uniqueLift` is stated for σ-semilinear maps `S.Field j →ₛₗ[t] V`, with t : ℂ →+* ℂ arbitrary. It is consumed with t = complex conjugation by `YM.DiffPlan.unionAdj`, the involution on the union that C04's `input:calibrated-actions` uses ("the same spaces on which … the involution … act", C04 ledger E4). The manuscript proof works verbatim for additive F_d with F_d(cv) = σ(c)F_d(v) for a fixed ring endomorphism σ of ℂ. One sentence saying so would let the later involution lift cite this proposition instead of re-proving it. Involutions remain out of scope, as the subsection says.

**O2 (optional, correspondence record).** (a) Record that (154) is the slot-0 instance of `YM.Dash.MonitorOps.Wakes.keep`, which holds at every slot of every `Shot`. The manuscript therefore assumes less than the source provides, and slot 0 is the instance `readies` uses. (b) The record could cite `YM.Dash.sameWake` and `YM.Dash.matchReg` (`Lean/YM/Dash/Slew.lean`; used at `Lean/YM/Org/Input.lean` l.108). This is the source's only transfer of a `Wakes` receipt between tables, and it requires agreement of the two tables on generic configurations: it serves the smoothing step `YM.Dash.awake`, not a phase. For a selected phase T with different correlations, `habits : P.Inclusions T` is therefore a genuinely separate premise of `YM.Bid.Pantry`. This supports the subsection's closing caveat.
