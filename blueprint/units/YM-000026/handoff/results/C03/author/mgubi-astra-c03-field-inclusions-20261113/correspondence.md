## What lifts_model needs before basis assembly

Fix one family C, table S and extraction order f. Its input `YM.Dash.Live S` gives integrability of the tested kernels; smoothness on separated configurations and compact test support supply this in the manuscript setting. `YM.Extraction.Order.Ordered` supplies the finite-cutoff response convergence on that same f and S. A `YM.Dash.Bin S` is a grade-indexed family of `YM.Dash.Branch` data: finite-coordinate and limiting idempotent projections. Operator-norm convergence is the separate `YM.Dash.Bin.Upper.slides` assumption, namely `YM.Dash.Bin.Matrix` / `YM.Dash.approaches`. `YM.Dash.Bin.Upper` and `YM.Dash.Bin.shortage` identify the kernel of that limiting projection with the all-Shot monitor by `YM.Dash.Bin.exactC`.

For each inclusion request, `YM.Dash.Spans.r` requires one common finite-cutoff target-label family x, independent of the Shot. `YM.Dash.Added.meets` says every actual tested response of x tends to the requested response. `YM.Dash.Added.costly` says the removed-component response, divided by 1 plus the norm of the projected x, tends to zero for every Shot. The denominator is tied to that same x and projection; pointwise approximants selected separately for different tests do not meet this hypothesis.

The inspected proof of `YM.Dash.brace` normalizes the projected labels by b=1/(1+norm(projected x)). It takes one cluster ultrafilter of the bounded pair (b,b projected x). All fixed-Shot limits restrict to that one refinement, so the limiting pair (c,v) satisfies G_i(v)=c Q_i for every i. Projection convergence gives P(v)=v. If c=0, exact monitor-kernel identification gives P(v)=0, hence v=0, contradicting norm(v)+c=1. Therefore v/c is one target label realizing every Q_i. This source argument does not require a countable enumeration of all tests. The manuscript assumes the output for basis labels; it does not purport to have produced these normalized approximation and projector premises for the actual selected table.
