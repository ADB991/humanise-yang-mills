## Canonical proof reuse and exact strengthening

The source inclusion statement uses original quotient representatives, whereas the new manuscript statement spells out every raw spectator. The bridge is `ex-phase-original-null`: changing an original representative adds an original null label, which every phase annihilates. The map K itself is linear by the earlier fixed-basis construction; a chosen representative section need not be linear. The source quotient map agrees with the induced map by its explicit lift formula (and uniqueness of response-preserving quotient maps). Thus no new assumption about a raw section is introduced.

The already-written `ex-phase-original-null`, `ex-compression-values`, `ex-phase-word` and the dense slab construction are invoked with the same S and Hilbert space. The new step compares two words whose distinguished grades differ; it does not treat their difference as a same-grade null label. Equality is obtained at operator level first. All characters then inherit it, before any null equality or phase selection is invoked. Only continuity of phase values is needed for the compact-test conclusion; `ex-phase-caps` supplies more regularity under its separate hypotheses. The quotient/union proof needs the established response identity, multilinearity and symmetry, not a fresh differentiability proof.

The conditional analytic attachment is exactly the inclusion-only chain already matched in PR274. Calibration, raw spaces, normalized law, mesh-volume factors and the ordered filter remain those of the same finite-volume family. It neither replaces the all-test monitor by finite rank tests nor extracts a different representative for each spectator. Original-null containment and optional equality are separate from the analytic supplier obligations. No source edits, Lean run, transitive axiom audit or independent acceptance is claimed.
