## Fixed dictionary and quantifiers

Use C for the actual finite-volume family, A_d=C.label d for its raw reference coefficient space, X_{d,c} for its terminal calibration, and c=(N,p) for cutoff pairs. Let f be one extraction, retained under any common refinement. The filter is f.fuse: for every sufficiently large selected period, then every sufficiently large selected mesh with threshold allowed to depend on that period. It is not a diagonal N=N(p) and not uniform in p. For each grade use the finite projection A_{d,c} and limiting projection P_d of finite-rank-kernel-transport (where the latter is called T_d). Avoid confusing P_d with a table or with that chapter's calibration L_c.

Fix the table S as the same smooth symmetric realization of the ordered correlations of C. For an arbitrary Shot θ (any finite arity with a distinguished slot, any spectator grades/labels, any compact smooth test supported in Conf), write f_{θ,c} for its finite-cutoff smeared functional and f_θ for its functional on S. The normalization is the mesh-volume factor a_N^{4n} and the normalized Wilson law of the same C at (N,p). The inclusion request has one fixed d≤e and a∈A_d; its approximating row x_c∈A_e must be selected before θ. There is no upper bound on the spectators' grades or arity. The empty spectator tuple corresponds to a one-slot Shot, not to a nonexistent distinguished slot in a zero-point word.
