## Transport rule (T)

Let C: ℝ⁴ → ℝ⁴, C(t, **x**) = (**x**, t), map C04 (time-first) coordinates to the public (time-last) coordinates of `statement.tex` l.24–27. C is linear with |det C| = 1. With η_first = diag(1,−1,−1,−1), the public form t² − |**x**|² has matrix η_last = C η_first Cᵀ. For a C04 Lorentz matrix Λ, the public one is C Λ C⁻¹, and the public translation by a is the C04 translation by C⁻¹a. Define the public objects from the C04 objects by

- Φ_pub(A, f) := Φ(v, f∘C) for f ∈ 𝒮(ℝ⁴), where v is the C04 union label of A (see F2);
- U_pub(a, Λ) := U(C⁻¹a, C⁻¹ΛC);
- P_pub := C_* P for the joint translation spectral measure.

Then f ↦ f∘C is a topological linear isomorphism of 𝒮(ℝ⁴) that commutes with complex conjugation. It maps the support of f to C⁻¹(supp f). Two sets are spacelike separated for η_last exactly when their C⁻¹-images are spacelike separated for η_first. The pullback satisfies (f∘C)∘(C⁻¹gC)⁻¹ = (f∘g⁻¹)∘C for every affine map g. C maps V̄₊ = {p⁰ ≥ |**p**|} onto {(**p**, E): |**p**| ≤ E}. The change of variables in an integral costs no Jacobian factor. These are the substitutions that re-review #91 issue 1 asks C04 to state. The outline already records them (`argument-outline.tex`: "η_last = C η_first Cᵀ and Λ_last = C Λ_first C⁻¹; the cone is transported by the same map. The ordering of the tube is unchanged").

### F3. Existence of a nonzero smeared field: argument and missing statement

The source proves `Reconstructed.acts` as follows (`HierarchyBridge.lean` l.85–88). The grade-0 unit class `S.unit 0` is nonzero by `YM.Table.alive` (`YM/Euclid/Null.lean` l.102–109, under `S.Basic` supplied by `F.room.kernel.laws`). Its image under the injective level map is nonzero by `S.pin_inj 0`. Faithfulness of the realization (`F.realization.qt.faithful`) then gives a test f with Φ(unit, f) ≠ 0. In mathematical terms:

*Inputs.* (U1) At some grade d there is a unit token 1_d whose insertion is the constant function 1. Its correlation laws are the source's `Table.Basic.unital` and `.trivial` (`YM/Euclid/Table.lean` l.75–78): inserting 1_d at any point of a distinct configuration does not change the correlation of the remaining word, and the empty word has correlation 1. These laws are for the **original** limiting table that defines the field levels. (U2) The level map E_d → E is injective (part of E4). (U3) `prop:boundary-detection`, which uses the null-equality input.

*Argument.* Suppose the class u_d of 1_d in E_d were zero. Then 1_d lies in the null space N_d, so its correlation against every exterior word vanishes, in particular against the empty exterior word at a single point x. By (U1) this correlation equals the correlation of the empty word, which is 1. This is a contradiction, so u_d ≠ 0. By (U2) its image v ∈ E is nonzero. By (U3) there is f ∈ 𝒮 with Φ(v, f) ≠ 0. Under (T), Φ_pub(A, f∘C⁻¹) = Φ(v, f) ≠ 0, where A is the public label of v. ∎

The manuscript states none of (U1) as a property of the limiting original table. `limits.tex` `prop:ex-limits-euclidean` carries "normalization" (l.222–224) but not the unit law. Unit laws appear only in later C14/C15 settings (`actual-stress-normalization.tex` l.17–18, `physical-ward-propagation.tex` l.493, `trace-branch-moments.tex` l.20). The lattice identity behind (U1) is immediate, since the unit insertion is the constant 1, and it passes to the ordered limits like the other exact identities in `prop:ex-limits-euclidean`. It still needs to be stated and proved where the limiting table is built. **Proposed owner:** the C03 limits/hierarchy author should add the unit law to the identities passed to the limit, and the C04 author should add the argument above as a corollary after `prop:boundary-detection`. That corollary is about five lines and needs no new analysis. Until then the clause rests on the source alone, and the C04 ledger's description of the gap (C04-to-C16-endpoint row, review M3) remains accurate.
